JeevaRaksha Privacy Policy & Data Handling Disclosure

Version: v13.1.3-academic-prototype  |  Updated: November 24, 2026
Repository: https://github.com/ullaslsmo-ship-it/jeevaraksha-emergency-assistance

This privacy policy accurately describes how the JeevaRaksha academic prototype stores, processes, displays, and shares data on your device. It does not make absolute privacy promises that browser storage cannot guarantee.

1. What Information Is Stored Locally (Unencrypted Browser Storage)

Information you enter—such as name, age, blood group, allergies, medical conditions, medications, emergency contacts (Safety Circle), optional district/vehicle details, and UI settings—is stored locally in your browser using localStorage and IndexedDB.

  • Not Automatically Encrypted: Browser localStorage and IndexedDB are plaintext browser storage. Anyone with unlocked access to your phone, computer, or browser extensions may be able to read stored data.
  • No Developer Database Server: This static prototype does not operate a developer-owned backend database for your medical profiles. When hosted on a static web host (such as GitHub Pages), standard HTTP access logs (e.g., IP address and page requests) are governed by the hosting provider.

2. Public Medical QR Codes Are NOT Encrypted

Important QR Risk Disclosure: Ordinary text and compact structured QR codes generated by JeevaRaksha are NOT encrypted. Anyone who scans, photographs, or copies your QR card or lockscreen wallpaper can read every field you selected. All QR consent toggles default to OFF, and v13.1 blocks medical data from being placed in URL query parameters. Do not include national ID numbers, PAN numbers, passwords, financial details, or full home addresses in public QR codes.

3. Voluntary External Sharing & Third-Party Services

  • SMS, WhatsApp & Phone Calls: When you intentionally tap a call, SMS, or WhatsApp action and confirm, JeevaRaksha opens your device dialer or external messaging app with your chosen recipient and message. Delivery and privacy are then governed by your telecom carrier or WhatsApp. Opening a composer does not mean the message was delivered.
  • External Maps & Government Portals: Tapping Directions (Online) opens Google Maps (requires internet), and tapping government health portal links opens external websites subject to their respective privacy policies.

4. Device Permissions (Location & Camera)

  • Location (GPS): Requested only after you explicitly tap a GPS button. Requires browser permission and a secure context (HTTPS or localhost). Manual State/District selection is available offline without GPS. Third-party IP geolocation fallbacks (GeoJS / IPWho.is) are removed.
  • Camera: Requested only when you explicitly start live QR scanning. Requires a compatible browser and secure HTTPS/localhost context (downloaded local file:// HTML files may block live camera access; use QR photo upload or manual text paste instead). Camera streams stop when scanning closes or the page is hidden.

5. Backups, Data Deletion & Limitations

  • JSON Backups: Exported JSON backup files are unencrypted plain-text files saved to your device Downloads folder—store and share them carefully.
  • Data Deletion: Using Settings & Privacy → Erase Device Data removes application keys from localStorage, clears sessionStorage, and attempts to delete the JeevaRakshaOfflineDB IndexedDB database. Deletion cannot revoke printed CR80 cards, lockscreen screenshots, previously exported JSON files, or messages already sent to external apps.

6. Medical & Non-Diagnostic Notice

JeevaRaksha does not provide medical diagnoses, prescribe medication dosages, or replace professional medical advice or emergency services. All saved or scanned medical profiles remain USER-REPORTED unless independently verified by healthcare personnel. In any emergency in India, call 108 or 112 immediately.
